How We Work

Good work needs room to think and a way to move forward together.

“Make the work clear enough for someone else to continue.”

  • Document decisions

    Write down the context, the choice, and what comes next.

  • Give specific feedback

    Talk about the work. Explain what could be clearer and why.

  • Protect focus time

    Leave space for thoughtful work, with clear handovers when help is needed.

  • Keep meetings purposeful

    Start with a question to resolve. Finish with a decision or a next step.
